What the score measures

  • Skill coverage (45) — how many of the posting's required skills appear on your resume.
  • Sections (25) — summary, experience, projects, skills, education, certifications.
  • Quantified results (15) — numbers tied to impact ($, %, 3x, "cut load time 38%").
  • Length (15) — full marks in the 400–1000 word range.

A coaching tool, not a real applicant tracking system. Scores are deterministic and relative to the lexicon in skills.py.
