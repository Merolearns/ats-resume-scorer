Data Analyst — Growth Analytics (fictional posting)

Northwind Outfitters is hiring a Data Analyst to join our Growth Analytics
team. You will partner with product and marketing to measure experiments,
build dashboards, and guide roadmap decisions with data.

Responsibilities
- Design and analyze A/B tests; communicate results to stakeholders.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau and Power BI.
- Write clean, documented SQL against our Snowflake data warehouse.
- Partner with data engineering on dbt models and data quality checks.

Required qualifications
- 3+ years of experience in data analysis, ideally in e-commerce.
- Strong SQL and experience with Python (pandas) for analysis.
- Experience with statistical methods: hypothesis testing and regression.
- Familiarity with the modern data stack: Snowflake, dbt, Airflow.
- Excellent stakeholder communication and storytelling skills.

Nice to have
- Experience with machine learning or forecasting.
- Exposure to AWS or GCP.
- Knowledge of experimental design and causal inference.
